01

Overview

Executive summary

XO Platform is a cloud system that runs a nursery from end to end. It brings admissions, child records, attendance and safe pickup, daily reports, billing and payments, staff management, events and parent communication into one product, in Arabic and English, on any device.

15Business modules
4Dedicated portals
6User roles
25Permission-controlled features
The problem

Nurseries run on scattered tools

  • Attendance on paper, and no record of who picked a child up.
  • Parent communication spread across personal WhatsApp groups.
  • Fees tracked in spreadsheets and cash collected with no trail.
  • Late pickups and extra hours that are never billed.
  • Parents who see nothing of their child's day.
The solution

One platform, one source of truth

  • Every child, family, class, invoice and staff member in one place.
  • QR check-in and check-out with a full audit trail.
  • Automatic recurring billing and late-pickup charges.
  • A parent app with daily reports, photos, invoices and chat.
  • Each person sees only what their role allows.

Three promises

Run the nursery

Admissions, classes, attendance, daily care, staff and operations are handled in guided screens instead of paper and spreadsheets.

Delight parents

Families follow their child's day in real time, pay online, sign permission slips and talk to teachers in one app.

Control the business

Owners see revenue, collection, attendance and staff at a glance, and decide who can do what.

Built for the region

Full Arabic right-to-left interface, Egyptian pounds (EGP), local payment methods (InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer and cash), and national-ID-based pickup verification.

01

Admissions & enrollment

Turn inquiries into enrolled, paying children through a guided online journey.

Key features

  • Public inquiry form for each nursery, with inquiry tracking and a waitlist.
  • Online parent sign-up and a multi-step application form.
  • Customisable registration templates: admins choose the steps, their names, icons and order, and add custom questions with answer types and validation.
  • Document upload and review, with clear statuses: draft, submitted, under review, documents pending, approved or rejected.
  • During the application, parents choose a tuition package and billing period (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual), plus an optional extra-hours package, and pay all or part before approval.
  • Approval enrolls the child and starts the recurring tuition subscription in one step.
  • A staff-side enrollment wizard for walk-in families.
  • Bulk import of existing children from CSV or Excel, with AI-assisted column matching, a review screen and progress tracking.

Who uses it

Branch adminManagerParent

Business value

Faster intake with no paperwork. Payment is collected before a seat is confirmed, and the nursery can migrate from spreadsheets in minutes.

04

Attendance & safe pickup

Know exactly who is in the building, who collected each child, and bill the extra time.

Key features

  • QR check-in and check-out: staff scan the parent's QR code with the scanner built into any phone.
  • Every scan records who scanned, the method (parent QR, one-time pickup QR or manual) and the time, in a full audit log.
  • Authorised pickups and one-time delegate pickup QR codes that carry the delegate's ID card (front and back). The code is used up when the pickup is confirmed.
  • Live attendance dashboard, KPIs, scan logs and per-child attendance reports.
  • Parent absence reports, automatic pickup reminders before closing time and a nightly "missing check-out" review.
  • Automatic late-pickup billing by the nursery's rules: grace period, 60/30/15-minute rounding, daily cap, optional early-arrival billing, and daily or monthly invoicing. Extra-hours packages are used first, and admins can correct or waive charges.
  • Staff attendance tracking.

Who uses it

AdminTeacherParent

Business value

Peace of mind for parents and a defensible record for the nursery. Extra hours that used to be forgotten are now billed automatically.

Packages, pricing & deals

Flexible pricing and promotions that turn interest into enrollment.

Key features

  • Fixed tuition packages with price, included features and daily hours taken automatically from the nursery's opening and closing times.
  • Billing periods per package (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual), priced automatically from the monthly price and adjustable by hand.
  • Extra-hours packages: unlimited or an hours quota, with a validity period, assigned to children.
  • Packages are activated or deactivated, never deleted, to protect billing history. Each package shows how many children are on it.
  • Deals & discounts: percentage or fixed-amount offers with an optional start and end date. Assign an existing deal to a package, or create a one-off custom discount from the package editor.
  • Parents see the discount, the original and new price, and a live countdown while applying. The discount applies to the first (admission) invoice.
  • Searchable, filterable tables of packages and deals, showing which packages and children each deal reaches.

Who uses it

Branch adminFinanceParent

Business value

Pricing that matches how the nursery actually sells, and time-limited offers that create urgency and lift sign-ups.

Customising access

Custom roles

Shape access to your organisation

Create roles such as "Floor Supervisor" or "Office Receptionist", choose their features, and grant view, create, update or delete per feature, with optional approval.

Each custom role is built on one of the base roles. It can hide features, but it can never reach more data than its base role allows.

Positions

Access that follows the job title

Each position, such as Lead Teacher or Head Cook, is linked to a role. When HR onboards a staff member into a position, the right access is applied automatically.

Built-in positions and roles are protected. Nurseries add their own on top.

How it feels

Key journeys

Journey A

From inquiry to enrolled child

  1. Inquiry. A family fills in the nursery's public inquiry form. If no seat is free, the family joins the waitlist.
  2. Sign up and apply. The parent creates an account and completes the nursery's own registration steps and documents.
  3. Choose a package. The parent picks a tuition package and billing period and sees any live deal with its countdown.
  4. Pay. The admission invoice is created. The parent pays all or part, and finance confirms the payment.
  5. Review. The nursery reviews the application and documents.
  6. Approve. One click enrolls the child and starts the recurring tuition subscription.
Journey B

A day at the nursery

  1. Arrival. The teacher scans the parent's QR code and the parent is notified of check-in.
  2. The day. The teacher posts photos for approval and writes the daily report.
  3. Live view. The director watches attendance and health alerts on the dashboard.
  4. Pickup reminder. Parents of children still in the building are reminded before closing.
  5. Pickup. The parent's or a delegate's QR code is scanned, and the system logs who collected the child.
  6. Late? Extra time is billed automatically by the nursery's rules.

08

Trust

Security, privacy and trust

Nurseries hold some of the most sensitive data there is: information about young children. XO protects it at every layer.

Each nursery's data is sealed off

Isolation is enforced inside the database itself, not only in the app, so one nursery can never read another's records. Automated cross-tenant denial checks verify this.

Role-based access everywhere

The interface hides what a role cannot use, and the database refuses it anyway.

Audit trails

Every attendance scan records who, how and when. Role changes are logged, and payments keep a full history of attempts, confirmations and rejections.

Privacy controls for media

Photos can require approval before parents see them, are visible only to the families of the children in them, and are subject to per-day limits.

Safe money handling

Payments are locked and balance-checked, and protected against double clicks and retries. Parents cannot pay against someone else's invoice.

Backups and recovery

Per-nursery data exports, a documented backup and recovery process, and an incident runbook.

Hosting

XO runs on managed cloud infrastructure: Supabase (PostgreSQL, authentication, storage and realtime) and Vercel for the web application, served over HTTPS.

The feature catalogue describes product capabilities. A capability in the codebase does not necessarily mean its external provider is connected or that it is enabled in every deployment; the distinctions below are based on the repository documentation and implementation.

CapabilityStatusDetails

Connect and verify messaging providers, secrets, delivery callbacks, failure handling and opt-out rules.

Define privacy, consent and operational requirements before estimating CCTV or transport user experiences.

Repository-specific readiness items to include in the plan

Quality baseline

The repository currently has no configured test script or matching unit-test files. An earlier npm run typecheck run on 8 October 2026 reported 115 TypeScript errors across 17 source files. Re-run the checks at project kickoff, record the new baseline, and agree remediation priorities before calling the application release-ready.

Integration scope

The codebase contains email/SMS/WhatsApp provider stubs, a Paymob client that returns an empty checkout URL, and camera/transport schema without complete user-facing workflows. Treat these as explicit work packages or exclusions—not completed launch features.

Go-live exit checklist

Business readiness
  • Nursery owner, admin, finance and class leads are trained and know escalation contacts.
  • Representative children, guardians, classes, authorized pickups, packages and balances are reconciled.
  • Role tests confirm each user sees only permitted features and records.
  • Families receive clear instructions for login, attendance, payments and consent.
Technical readiness
  • Production environment, secrets, backups, migration state and rollback plan are verified.
  • Critical routes and workflows pass smoke tests; errors and monitoring owners are defined.
  • External providers are explicitly marked connected, stubbed or out of launch scope.
  • Support, incident response, data export and recovery procedures are available.
Planning assumptions

One pilot nursery, an available business decision-maker, timely data and user access, a named implementation owner, and no major net-new module build are assumed. The 12-week estimate covers configuration and rollout of capabilities already represented in the repository plus agreed validation and defect remediation. If production-readiness work reveals substantial missing implementation, unstable external providers, migration/data-quality issues, or unplanned scope (including Paymob, messaging, transport or CCTV), re-estimate the affected wave before committing dates.
